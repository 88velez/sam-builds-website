If you have ever shopped around for a website for your contracting business, shop, or restaurant, you have likely run into the exact same sales pitch:

An agency shows you a polished PowerPoint presentation, lists their packages, demands a 50% upfront deposit, and then asks you to wait three to six weeks while they "design" your site.

We think that model is completely broken.

## The Upfront Payment Trap

When you pay thousands of dollars upfront to a designer, you are taking on 100% of the risk. You are paying for a promise. 

If they deliver a site that doesn't align with your branding, feels cheap, or loads too slowly, you are stuck. You've already spent your deposit, and getting out of the contract is a legal nightmare. This upfront deposit model protects the agency’s cash flow while transferring all uncertainty directly onto the small business owner.

We don't think you should buy a website without seeing it first. You wouldn't buy a truck without driving it, and you shouldn't buy a digital storefront without testing the layout.

## Flipping the Risk: The 48-Hour Free Preview

We do business the other way around. 

When you get in touch with us:

1. **We spend 15 minutes talking** about your services, target audience, and preferences.
2. **Within 48 hours, we build a custom interactive preview** of your new homepage. 
3. **We send you a real link** that you can open on your personal phone, tablet, and computer.

This is not a generic static image or flat PDF mockup. It's a real, responsive web page coded from scratch with your colors, your services, and actual local copywriting. 

If you open the link and love it, we choose a flexible plan (Pay-in-Full or Monthly Subscription) and finish building the rest of the site. If you look at it and decide it's not the right fit, you walk away.
