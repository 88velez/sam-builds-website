## Why a blog matters

Search engines love fresh, keyword-rich content. A regularly updated blog is one of the strongest signals you can send Google that your site is active and authoritative.

## Tips for great posts

- Write for one specific person — your ideal customer.
- Target one keyword phrase per post.
- Aim for 800+ words on cornerstone topics.
- Include images with descriptive alt text.
- Internal-link to related services pages.
